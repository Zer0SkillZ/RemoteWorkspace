## Class Identity
The Monk focuses on traditions not just in combat, but as a life style; each tradition being finely
shaped by an Elder Spirit Walker. As a young monk, you follow the old pathways of the elders. You learn
the world through their ways; you come to see the world through their vision.

## Traditions (Augmentation Paths)

### Wilhelm's Transcendent Palm
A defensive martial tradition focused on technique and combinations.
Practitioners gain benefits from successfully executing combinations and become progressively more
difficult to overcome as combat continues.
The tradition emphasizes defense, control, and building advantages through proper technique execution.
- Can't use martial, or ranged weapons
- Can't adorn Armor
- Specializes in combo as stat(s) boosts

### Sun Tzu's Endless Staff
An offensive martial tradition focused on maintaining pressure through combinations.
Practitioners specialize in Quarterstaffs, Glaives, and Jo Staffs, using positioning, reach, and
aggressive technique chains to overwhelm opponents.
The tradition rewards offensive momentum and the ability to continuously apply pressure, often at the
cost of defense.
- Specializes in Quarter staffs, Glaves, and Jo Staffs
- Can't Adorn Armor
- Specializes in combo as attack pressure but at the cost of defense

### Zhuangzi's Butterfly Chains
A martial tradition that combines specialized weapon techniques with psionic abilities.
Practitioners specialize in Three Section Staffs, Chain Whips, and Kali Sticks, utilizing combinations
to interact with and enhance their psionic powers.
The tradition focuses on illusions, disruption, deception, and battlefield manipulation through a
combination of martial skill and mental influence.
- Specializes in Three Section Staffs, Chain whips, and Kali Sticks
- Can't adorn Armor
- Specializes in utilizing combo to interact with the psionics

### Aurelius' Unbroken Mountain
A durable martial tradition focused on resilience, endurance, and overwhelming force.
Practitioners forsake traditional martial and ranged weapons in favor of super-heavy armor and their own
physical strength. The tradition specializes in converting combinations into Internal Potential and
manifesting aspects of their Internal Summit, a unique metaphysical realm that reflects the
practitioner's personal growth and development. The tradition emphasizes deliberate action, durability,
and the ability to withstand what would break others.
- Can't use martial, or ranged weapons
- Trade two of their AP for RP
- Specializes in Super-heavy armor
- Specializes in converting combo into internal potential
- Specializes in manifesting their Internal Summit

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Standard class progression.
- Reaction Points (RP): Used primarily for defensive maneuvers, grapples, collision control, and
  momentum-based reactions.
- Bonus Points (BP): Fuel explosive movement, jumps, improvised techniques, and high-risk physical exertion.
- Combo: Granted when following the footsteps of your Spirit walker.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on First Class)
- Armor: light, Medium, Heavy
- Weapons: Variable
- Tool Mastery: Meditation Set, Camping equipment, Incense
- Stat Focus: Dexterity or Strength
