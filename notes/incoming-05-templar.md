## Class Identity (To be replaced)
Bound by fracture and sustained by graft, the Templar is a living vessel of a higher will. Their power
does not flow freely it is drawn, stored, and released with purpose. Each fracture of the Lock deepens the
bond between bearer and Luminarkon, shaping them into a conduit of divine, infernal, or void-touched force.

The Templar is a finite resource frontloaded endurance class. They do not regenerate power in combat.
Instead, they prepare, conserve, and unleash stored Chakra with precision. Their strength lies in
controlled bursts, marked pressure, and the gradual unraveling of the Lock.

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Standard class progression.
- Reaction Points (RP): Used primarily for defensive maneuvers, grapples, collision control, and
  momentum based reactions.
- Bonus Points (BP): Fuel explosive movement, jumps, improvised techniques, and high risk physical exertion.
- Chakra: A finite reservoir of grafted energy. Spent to empower abilities. Restored only during
  Stabilization, Light, and Heavy Rests.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on First Class)
- Armor: Light, Medium, Heavy
- Weapons: Martial weapons
- Tool Mastery: Religious Focuses, Holy scripture
- Stat Focus: Strength

## Core Nodes

### 1. Chakra Conduit
The graft awakens within you, a quiet ember waiting to be shaped.
You gain a Chakra Pool.
- Base Maximum Chakra 6
- You regain all Chakra during a Heavy Rest.
- You regain half your Maximum Chakra rounded down during a Light Rest.
- During a Stabilization Rest, you may meditate to regain HD as health and Chakra.
