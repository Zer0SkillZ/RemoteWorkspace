## Class Identity
The Scraper is an improvised mastermind. They carry no blade and draw no bow. The battlefield itself is
their arsenal. A chair, a stone, a steel rod, anything becomes a weapon in the Scraper's hands. They do
not circle a fight; they dive headlong into it, their own life wagered on every brutal exchange.

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Standard class progression.
- Reaction Points (RP): Used primarily for defensive maneuvers, grapples, collision control, and
  momentum-based reactions.
- Bonus Points (BP): Fuel explosive movement, jumps, improvised techniques, and high-risk physical exertion.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on First Class)
- Armor: Light, Medium (Scrapers may benefit from Heavy armor through class features rather than baseline
  proficiency.)
- Weapons: Improvised weapons, Unarmed strikes
- Tool Mastery: Salvager's Tools
- Stat Focus: Strength

## Core Nodes

### 1. Adaptability
You have the uncanny ability to use any item as a weapon.
In addition, you can use Strength for all improvised ranged weapons.

**Object Weight Mod**
- Light objects deal (3)(1)(2) damage with an AP cost of 1 AP.
- Medium objects deal (4)(2)(5) damage with an AP cost of 2 AP.
- Heavy objects deal (6)(6)(5) damage with an AP cost of 3 AP.
- Super Heavy objects deal (12)(2)(6) damage with an AP cost of 4 AP or 3AP + 1 Fatigue.

**Object Edge Mod**
- Blunt objects deal Bludgeoning damage unless they are Super Heavy, in which case they deal Impact damage.
- Pointed objects deal Puncture damage unless they are Super Heavy, in which case they deal Impact damage.
- Serrated or sharp objects deal Slashing damage.
- Jagged objects deal Bleed damage.
  - If the object is Light, it gains +2 Bleed damage.

**Object Material Mod**

*Wooden Objects — Brittle Bite (Specialization)*
The object gains a health pool based on its quality:
- Rubble: 1d6
- Mundane: 1d8
- Sturdy: 1d12
- Impeccable: 4d8
Due to its brittle nature, the object gains +2 damage while it is not at 1 HP.
When the object makes its final attack before breaking, it surges with power, dealing an additional
+4 (4)(4) damage before shattering.

*Stone Objects — Uneven Blunder (Specialization)*
When picked up, roll 1d8 to determine how unevenly weighted the object is.
The rolled number determines both how difficult the object is to use and how hard it hits.
- Apply the rolled number as a – to hit and a + to damage.

*Hard Metal Objects — Adaptable & Dependable (Specialization)*
The object can be affected by spells as if it were a traditional weapon.
For example, the Effervescent Flames spell may be applied, granting the weapon leaping flame effects.

### 2. Submission (2 AP)
You make a competing Brawn Check against a creature that is no more than one size larger than you, or
smaller, as you grapple them to the ground.
While the creature is grappled, you gain Medium Advantage on all melee attacks made against them.
At the start of the creature's turn, or any time you receive damage, the creature may make a Brawn Saving
Check against (3 + your Brawn) to attempt to break free.

### 3. Clobbering Machine
You find it just as effective to clobber your foes with your meaty fists.
- Your unarmed melee strikes deal 2 + half your Strength, to a maximum of 6.
- Your Critical and Epic Critical modifiers are equal to half your Brawn.

### 4. Monumental Jump
You may spend 1 BP to jump a distance equal to your footspeed.
This jump does not provoke opportunity attacks when passing through or over a creature.

### 5. Junk Javelin (2 AP, 1 BP)
If a piece of debris around you is long and straight, you can turn it into an Improvised Javelin.

Junk Javelin Damage
- Light: (5)(0)(0) — 1 AP
- Medium: (6)(0)(0) — 2 AP
- Heavy: (7)(0)(0) — 3 AP
- Super Heavy: (14)(0)(0) — 4 AP

The Junk Javelin does not gain bonus damage from Critical or Epic Critical hits.
However, the javelin becomes lodged in the target's body.
- Critical Hit: Shallow embed
- Epic Critical: Deep embed
Junk Javelins cannot be removed during combat and require a Stabilization Rest to extract.

**Deeply Embedded Junk Javelin**
- AC = 2 + the creature's AC
- On a successful melee hit, the javelin detonates into shrapnel, dealing:
  - Half scrapper damage (Half node progression + Brawn)
  - +1 damage per point your attack roll exceeds the javelin's AC

**Shallowly Embedded Junk Javelin**
- AC = 5 + the creature's AC
- On a successful melee hit, the javelin detonates into shrapnel, dealing:
  - Half scrapper damage (Half node progression + Brawn)
  - +1 damage per point your attack roll exceeds the javelin's AC

If two or more Junk Javelins are embedded in the same creature, destroying one causes all embedded
javelins to detonate simultaneously.
- Each additional Junk Javelin adds +3 bonus damage
- These bonus detonations do not gain damage from exceeding AC

### 6. Mayhem Specialist
Once per day, you can embrace the chaos of the battlefield.
For 1 minute:
- Difficult terrain is treated as normal terrain
- Normal terrain is treated as difficult terrain

### 7. Brawn and Bastion
**Might Is Right**
While wearing Heavy or Super Heavy Armor, your AC increases by half your Brawn modifier (maximum +6)

**Brawn and Grit**
While wearing no armor, your statistics change as follows:
- +35 ft to maximum movement speed
- EC = 5 + Dexterity + (2 Per 2,500 ascension)
- AC = 10 + Strength + (3 Per 2,500 ascension)
- Critical Resistance Modifier = Brawn Skill
- Epic Critical Resistance Modifier = half your Brawn or Dodge Skill
- Stance = Strength + Brawn + (5 Per 2,000 ascension)
- Arcane Resist = Dexterity + intelligence + 2

### 8. Mighty Hands
Your grip is strong and undeniable.
You can wield two-handed weapons in one hand without disadvantage.

### 9. Defensive Hands
While in combat, if you have at least one free hand, you are treated as if you have a +1 (1)(1) shield
equipped.
Additionally, once per round, you may spend 2 RP to attempt to reduce damage from an attack against you by
making a Resistance Skill Check equal to half the creature's total hit roll.

### 10. Free Bird
If you and another creature are falling alongside one another, you may attempt a competing Brawn Check.
- Success: You position the creature beneath you; they take half your fall damage.
- Failure: Nothing happens.
- Critical Failure (creature exceeds your roll by 5+): You take half their fall damage instead.

### 11. Violent Momentum
Once per turn, when you destroy, break, or discard an improvised weapon as part of an attack, you gain the
condition Momentum until the end of your next turn.

While you have Momentum:
- Your next melee attack gains Small Advantage
- Your next improvised weapon attack costs –1 AP (minimum 1)

Momentum is lost immediately if you:
- End a turn
- Become Staggered or flat-footed

### 12. Environmental Brutalist
You no longer suffer penalties for fighting in confined, unstable, or cluttered terrain.
- You ignore Small Disadvantage imposed by tight spaces, rubble, debris, or uneven ground.
- When you slam, throw, or collide a creature into terrain or objects, they take +2 bonus damage, when you
  reach level 20 in scraper, they instead take +4 bonus damage.

### 13. Improvised Mastery
Your understanding of chaos has become deliberate.
- Improvised weapons you wield gain +1 Critical Threshold.
- When you score a Critical Hit with an improvised weapon, you may choose:
  - The target suffers +3 Stance damage
  - The target gets pushed back 10ft
  - The target has pushed prone with on a 1d4 (4) 25%

### 14. The Havok in the rubble
If you take damage before your first attack in a round, you can use 1 RP to launch junk at the attacker,
dealing 2 damage per 2000 ascension score

### 15. Devotion to Junk
You believe in junk as a weapon so deeply that you can bless a piece of scrap for 1 BP, causing it to act
as a magic item.
- Its normal damage type is converted into its magical damage counterpart.

### 16. Conservation of Violence
*Force does not vanish—it transfers.*
When you hit a creature with a melee attack after moving at least 10 ft in a straight line toward it,
choose one of the following effects:
- The target takes +3 bonus damage and is pushed 5 ft directly away from you, or
- You convert the impact into internal stress, dealing +4 Stance damage instead of bonus damage
If the target collides with a solid object, creature, or terrain feature due to this push, they take an
additional 2 damage.
This effect may trigger once per turn.

### 17. Vector Lock (once per encounter)
You understand how to steal motion from others.
When a creature moves within your reach or past you, you may spend 1 RP to impose a Vector Lock until the
end of their movement. As you toss out scrap cow traps in the creatures path

While Vector Locked:
- If they change direction, they take 2 damage for each 5 ft moved after the change
- You gain one small advantage per 5ft the creature moved during the effect on your next attack, however
  you can't gain benefits from any other advantages during the benefits of vector lock.
Vector Lock may only affect one creature at a time.

### 18. Terminal Clinch
When you successfully grapple a creature, or maintain a grapple at the start of your turn, you may spend
2 AP to initiate a Terminal Clinch.

Choose one of the following maneuvers:

**Ground Breaker** — You drive the creature violently into the ground or a nearby surface.
- The creature takes half your scrapper damage (Half node progression + Brawn)
- The creature takes Stance damage equal to the damage dealt
- If the surface is breakable, it is destroyed, and the creature takes +3 bonus damage

**Momentum Reversal** — When a grappled creature attempts to move, shove, or escape, you may redirect
their force.
- The creature takes 2 damage per 5 ft of movement they attempted this turn
- Their movement immediately ends
- You may reposition both yourself and the creature up to 5 ft, ignoring difficult terrain

**Gravity Debt** — You can launch yourself and the grappled creature up 20ft landing them in a painful
position.
- Both creatures fall up to 20 ft (or the maximum available distance)
- The target takes full fall damage
- You take half fall damage
This fall does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Terminal Clinch may be used once per round per creature.

### 19. Unstoppable Mass
Once per combat, as a Free Action at the start of your turn, you may enter an Unstoppable state for 3
rounds.
While Unstoppable:
- You gain Resistance to Stance damage
- You ignore movement penalties from difficult terrain, forced movement, or being partially restrained
- The first time each round you would be Staggered, Pushed, or Knocked Prone, the effect is ignored

In addition, at the end of each of your turns while Unstoppable:
- If you hit at least one creature this turn, regain 1 BP
- If you grappled, shoved, or collided a creature this turn, regain 2 Stance

Unstoppable ends immediately if you are rendered Unconscious, or after 3 rounds

### 20. Pièce de Résistance (Once per Day)
You execute a flawless expression of improvised combat mastery.
When you activate Pièce de Résistance, choose one of the following attack modes. This choice is made at
activation and cannot be changed. You may only use one Pièce de Résistance per day.

**I. Crowning Blow (Improvised Weapon Attack)**
You seize a nearby object / junk, debris, or battlefield refuse, and deliver a singular, perfect strike.
- Make one melee attack using an improvised weapon.
- The attack deals 1.5x base damage.
- Treat the attack as both a Critical Hit and Epic Critical Hit.
- The target takes additional Stance damage equal to your Brawn modifier.
If the improvised weapon would normally break, it survives this attack, then immediately shatters
afterward. If this attack breaks the target's Stance, excess Stance damage spills over into HP as true
damage.

**II. Junkline Ballistic (Improvised Ranged Attack)**
You convert scrap into a terminal projectile.
- Choose a piece of debris within reach and make a ranged improvised weapon attack.
- The attack ignores cover, shield bonuses, and small sources of disadvantage.
- On hit, the projectile automatically embeds as if from Junk Javelin (Deep Embed).
Immediately after embedding, the projectile detonates:
- Deal half the target's creatures combat level as damage
- +1 damage per point your attack roll exceeds the target's AC
This detonation does not require a follow-up attack, and can force chain reactions from other spiers.

**III. Absolute Clinch (Wrestling / Grapple Attack)**
You commit to pure control—mass, leverage, and inevitability.
- Make a Brawn Check contested by the target's Brawn or Dodge (their choice).
- On success, you immediately grapple the target and execute a decisive maneuver. Choose one:
  - Ground Obliteration: The target takes your scrapper damage (10+Brawn) + full Stance damage, and is
    knocked Prone and Staggered.
  - Aerial Catastrophe: You and the target leap or fall up to 30 ft.
    - The target takes full fall damage + your Brawn modifier
    - You take no fall damage
On a pinacol success, out rolling the target by 8 or more you complete both actions.
On failure, your attack action will still deal half damage.
