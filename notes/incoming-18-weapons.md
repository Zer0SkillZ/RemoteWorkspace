## Notes for the build
- 54 weapon rows (some weapons have two rows, one per attack type: Long Sword, Hand Axe, Polearm, Scythe).
- Great Club appears in both One-Handed and Heavy Two-Handed with identical stats.
- "<Damage Buff>" (Great Club) is not in the weapon traits list.
- Ranged AP is written as "X–Y" (e.g. 3–3, 1–4); meaning not yet explained (perhaps attack AP – reload AP?).
- Every Specialized Talent except <Damage Buff> matches a trait in part 17.
- Damage types seen: Slashing, Piercing, Puncture, Bludgeon, Impact, Bleed.
