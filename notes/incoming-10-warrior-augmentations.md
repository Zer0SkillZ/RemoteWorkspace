# Incoming notes, part 10: Warrior Augmentations (all)
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept.)

Six Augmentation Paths (the sins): Wrath, Envy, Gluttony, Avarice, Acedia, Lust.
Each path has a variant for node 1 (War Cry) and node 2 (Champions Call • Mighty Pride).
Format per variant: tagline, ¤ ability, bullets, Flaw, closing quote.

## Node 1 — War Cry variants

### (1) Wrath
*The warrior who demands blood.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Wrath:
- Choose one enemy within your Presence when War Cry is activated.
- For the duration of the effect, your first successful attack each turn against that enemy is treated as
  if that creatures stance, and Arcane-resistance was broken; however your base damage is halved during the
  effect of your first attack.
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: If the chosen enemy is defeated by a creature other than yourself or leaves your Presence, the
  effect ends immediately, and you suffer a small disadvantage on your next turn.
> One of you will answer for the rest.

### (1) Envy
*The warrior who fixates on greatness.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Envy:
- When War Cry is activated, choose the strongest enemy (highest Ascension, CL, or AG discretion) within
  your Presence.
- While the effect lasts, you gain Small Advantage on attacks made only against that enemy, and they gain
  a small-disadvantage on any attack made on a creature other than yourself.
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: You suffer Small Disadvantage on attacks made against any other creature.
> Only you matter.

### (1) Gluttony
*The warrior who feeds on pain.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Gluttony:
- When War Cry is activated, you gain 1 Temporary HP for each enemy within your Presence.
- For the duration of the effect, each time you take damage, you gain 1 additional Temporary HP (At the
  End of the war cries effect).
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: You cannot gain Temporary HP from any other source while this effect is active.
> Hurt me again.

### (1) Avarice
*The warrior who hoards advantage.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Avarice:
- When War Cry is activated, you gain 1 stored edge for each enemy within your Presence (maximum 3).
- At any point during the duration, you may spend 1 stored edge to gain Small Advantage on an attack,
  check, or saving throw.
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: Any unspent stored edges are lost when the effect ends, and you take 3 True damage per unspent
  edge in a single damage instance.
> I will decide when it's worth it.

### (1) Acedia
*The warrior who endures without urgency.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Acedia:
- For the duration of the effect, while you remain stationary on your turn, you gain +1 AC.
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: If you move more than 5 feet on your turn willingly or otherwise, the effect immediately ends.
> Come to me. Or don't.

### (1) Lust
*The warrior who thrives on proximity and thrill.*
¤ Call of the Warrior's Lust:
- For the duration of the effect, the first time each turn you enter or remain within melee range of an
  enemy, gain +20 movement speed until the end of that turn.
- The effect lasts for a number of turns equal to the number of enemies within your Presence.
- Flaw: If you end your turn with no enemies within melee range, the effect ends immediately.
> (no closing quote given)

## Node 2 — Champions Call variants

### (2) Wrath
*The champion who must strike first.*
¤ Champion's Call of Wrath:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, choose one enemy within your Domain. On your first attack of the
  encounter against that enemy, you gain +2 to hit, plus +1 to hit per 2,500 Ascension Score.
- If this attack hits, it is treated as if the creature's Stance is already damaged by 2 before damage is
  applied.
- Flaw: If your first attack does not target the chosen enemy, or misses, the effect is lost and you
  suffer Small Disadvantage on your next attack.
> I will not be denied my opening.

### (2) Envy
*The champion who measures themselves against greatness.*
¤ Champion's Call of Envy:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, choose the strongest enemy within your Domain (highest
  Ascension, CL, or AG discretion).
- On your first attack of the encounter against that enemy, you gain Small Advantage, in addition to +2 to
  hit, plus +1 to hit per 2,500 Ascension Score.
- Flaw: If your first attack targets any other creature, you instead suffer Small Disadvantage on that
  attack.
> Only one of us deserves the title.

### (2) Gluttony
*The champion who hungers for the clash.*
¤ Champion's Call of Gluttony:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, your first attack of the encounter gains +2 to hit, plus +1 to
  hit per 2,500 Ascension Score.
- If that attack hits, you gain Temporary HP equal to half your Brawn (rounded up).
- If that attack misses, you instead take 2 True Damage.
- Flaw: You cannot gain Temporary HP from this effect if you do not engage an enemy on your first turn.
> Feed me the impact.

### (2) Avarice
*The champion who hoards certainty.*
¤ Champion's Call of Avarice:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, you gain +2 to hit, plus +1 to hit per 2,500 Ascension Score, on
  your first attack of the encounter.
- You may choose to delay this benefit until any later attack during the encounter.
- Flaw: If the encounter ends before you use this benefit, you take 5 True Damage for holding back.
> I will strike when it profits me.

### (2) Acedia
*The champion who does not rush.*
¤ Champion's Call of Acedia:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, you gain +2 to hit, plus +1 to hit per 2,500 Ascension Score, on
  your first attack made while stationary.
- This benefit remains and is reusable until you move more than 5 feet on your turn.
- Flaw: If you move more than 5 feet at any point, the effect is lost.
> If they want me, they will come.

### (2) Lust
*The champion who lives for the opening.*
¤ Champion's Call of Lust:
- At the start of an encounter, for 1 BP, you gain +2 to hit, plus +1 to hit per 2,500 Ascension Score, on
  your first attack made against a creature within melee range.
- If this attack hits, you may immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking reactions from that
  creature.
- Flaw: If your first attack is made at range, the effect is lost.
> Be close. Be seen.
