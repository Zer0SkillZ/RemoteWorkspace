## Class Identity (To be replaced)
The Warrior is an engagement anchor and frontline dominator, built to control space, punish retreat, and
convert risk into overwhelming physical pressure. Through stance-breaking force, relentless momentum, and
calculated overcommitment, the Warrior turns proximity into advantage and thrives where the fight is
thickest.

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Standard class progression.
- Reaction Points (RP): Used primarily for defensive maneuvers, grapples, collision control, and
  momentum-based reactions.
- Bonus Points (BP): Fuel explosive movement, jumps, improvised techniques, and high-risk physical exertion.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on First Class)
- Armor: light, Medium, Heavy
- Weapons: Martial weapons
- Tool Mastery: Fire making kit, Camping equipment
- Stat Focus: Strength

### 3. Blood Before Shame
*Pain is preferable to weakness, and blood is the perfume of the warrior.*
Passive
When you take damage during your turn, or after committing to an attack, from a creature, you gain 1
temporary AP on your next turn.
However, if the creature deals an Epic Critical Hit against you, you may choose to make your next attack
with Medium Advantage instead of gaining the temporary AP.

### 4. Refusal to Yield — Fracture Node
*Pride does not retreat. When you commit to a fight, you deny your enemy the luxury of leaving it.*
You may only benefit from one Disengage Fracture at a time.

#### Fractal Path 1 — Unarmed: Grip of Defiance
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
When a creature you are engaged with attempts to disengage or forcibly leave your melee range, you may
spend 1 RP to contest them, seizing their weapon as they try to pull away.
Make a Brawn Check, contested by the creature's Brawn or Stability (their choice).
- On a success: The creature's movement for that action is reduced to 0, and they remain engaged with you.
- On a failure: The creature disengages successfully, but their movement is reduced by 5 feet for the
  remainder of the turn.
You must have at least one free hand to use this effect.

#### Fractal Path 2 — One-Handed Weapon: Hooked Withdrawal
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
When a creature you are engaged with attempts to disengage or forcibly leave your melee range, you may
spend 1 RP to interrupt their movement, catching them with the hooked pressure of your weapon.
Make an Attack Roll against the creature's AC.
- On a hit: The creature disengages successfully, but their movement is reduced by 5 feet, and they
  suffer Small Disadvantage on their next attack made before the end of their next turn.
- On a miss: The creature disengages normally.
You must be wielding a one-handed melee weapon to use this effect.

#### Fractal Path 3 — Two-Handed Weapon: Crippling Retreat
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
When a creature you are engaged with attempts to disengage or forcibly leave your melee range, you may
spend 1 RP to punish their retreat, crushing or piercing their foot as they try to escape.
Make an Attack Roll against the creature's AC.
- On a hit: The creature disengages successfully, but until the end of your next turn:
  - They take +2 Stance Damage for each instance of damage they receive from you or your allies, and
  - They take 1 True Damage for every Pace they move.
- On a miss: The creature disengages normally.
You must be wielding a two-handed melee weapon to use this effect.

#### Fractal Path 4 — Shield: Stand the Line
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
- When a creature you are engaged with attempts to disengage or forcibly leave your melee range, you may
  spend 1 RP to block their escape with your shield.
- The creature's disengage attempt fails, and they remain engaged with you.
- Stand the line is still reactively available even after further fracturing.
You must be wielding a shield to use this effect.

##### Fractal Path 4 — Buckler Discipline — Redirecting Halt
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
- When you successfully prevent a creature from disengaging using Stand the Line, you may choose to allow
  the disengage to succeed instead.
If you do, the creature immediately leaves your melee range, but their movement ends immediately after
doing so (1 Pace.).

##### Fractal Path 4 — Crest Shield Discipline — Anchored Denial
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
- When you successfully prevent a creature from disengaging using Stand the Line, the creature suffers
  halved movement speed until the end of their Next turn.

##### Fractal Path 4 — Tower Shield Discipline — Absolute Wall
1RP | Cooldown 3 Rounds | Cost: 250 Ascension
- When you use Stand the Line, the disengage attempt automatically fails. No roll or contest is required.
This effect can only be used once per round.

### 5. Pushed Too Far
*When you press beyond reason and find yourself surrounded, you don't retreat—you force space.*

#### Bullhead Charge
Cost: 1 AP | 1 BP
You lower your head and drive forward, attempting to smash through the creature blocking your escape.
Make a competing Brawn Check against the targeted creature.
If you are at or below half HP, you gain Small Advantage on this check.
- On a success: You force your way past the creature, knocking them Prone, and immediately disengage
  from them. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks from the targeted creature.
- On a failure: You fail to break through their space. The creature gains Small Disadvantage on their
  next attack made against you.

#### In Too Deep
Cost: 1 AP | 1 BP
You attempt to escape by vaulting over, climbing onto, or bounding across the creatures surrounding you.
The maximum number of creatures you can jump over is equal to your Traversal Modifier + 1, to a minimum
of 1, compared against the Horde Density of the creatures you are attempting to cross.

Horde Density Values:
| Size Category | Distance Modifier |
|---|---|
| Puny | 1 |
| Small | 1 |
| Medium | 2 |
| Large | 2 |
| Massive | 3 |
| Gargantuan | 4 |
| Colossal | 5 |

If your allowed number of creatures meets or exceeds the required Horde Density, you successfully escape
the horde without provoking disengage-based reactions from those creatures.

#### Making Space
1 AP | 1 BP
You swing, shove, and bash outward, forcing the creatures around you to give ground.
When you take this Bonus Action, all creatures adjacent to you are pushed 5 feet directly away from you.
Creatures smaller than you are pushed 10 feet instead.
To successfully push the horde back, you must make a successful Attack Roll against the horde's
Resistance Value.

Horde Resistance Values:
| Size Category | Horde Resistance Modifier |
|---|---|
| Puny | 0 |
| Small | 0 |
| Medium | 1 |
| Large | 2 |
| Massive | 3 |
| Gargantuan | 5 |
| Colossal | 5 |

On a success, the affected creatures are pushed as described. On a failure, no movement occurs.

### 7. Press Your Luck
*You push forward, daring the fight to break before you do.*
Passive
At the end of each of your turns, if you attempted at least one attack while engaged with one or more
enemies, you gain 1 Pressure, up to a maximum of 3.

**Pressure** — For each point of Pressure, you gain +1 to hit on melee attacks.

**Losing Pressure** — You lose all accumulated Pressure if any of the following occur:
- You do not attempt an attack during your turn
- You use an effect that forcibly removes you from engagement
- If Pressure is lost because you did not attempt an attack, you take 2 Stance Damage per point of
  Pressure lost.
- Pressure lost for any other reason you take 1 Stance Damage per point of Pressure lost.
When Pressure is lost, your Pressure is set to 0.

### 8. Iron Momentum
Once Per Turn
*Forward motion is pressure made visible.*
Once per turn, when you move at least 1 Pace directly toward a creature and hit it with a melee attack
during the same turn:
- The attack deals 3 True Damage.
- If you spent at least 1 BP during that turn, it instead deals +5 True Damage.
- If you move one or more Stride(s), you can also push them back 1 Stride if they fail a Stability,
  Dodge, Acrobatics, or Athletics (Creatures Choice) of half the hit modifier to a (max of 15)

### 9. Battlefield Gravity
*Where you stand, the fight holds.*
Creatures engaged with you suffer Small disadvantage to disengage-related rolls and contested checks made
to leave your melee range.

**Temporary Fortification** — Once per encounter: If two or more creatures are engaged with you at the
start of your turn, you gain +10 Stance Resistance until the start of your next turn.

### 10. All In
*When you commit fully to an attack, you leave yourself dangerously exposed.*
Base Effect (Core Node): Cool Down (1 Round(s))
- When you use All In, your AC is reduced to your EC until your next turn, as if your stance were broken.
- All In allows you to add 1 additional AP to a single attack.
This effect applies regardless of fracture choices.

#### Fractal Path 1
(1) Lesser Fracture: All In (Light) — Cost: 250 Ascension
For every AP added to your attack through All In, you gain: +2 Damage; +1 Critical Threshold; +1 Epic
Critical Threshold. *Raw impact at extreme personal risk.*

Calculated Overextension — (2) Greater Fracture: All In (Light) — Cost: 500 Ascension
When using All In (Light):
- The AC reduction lasts until the end of the current round of combat, instead of until your next turn.
- If the attack results in a Critical Hit or higher, you gain +1 Temp RP.
*Timing and accuracy over brute force.*

#### Fractal Path 2
Lesser Fracture: All In (Medium) — Cost: 250 Ascension
For every AP added to your attack through All In, you gain: +4 Damage; +2 Critical Threshold; +1 Epic
Critical Threshold. *Raw impact at extreme personal risk.*

Relentless Commitment — Greater Fracture: All In (Medium) — Cost: 500 Ascension
When using All In (Medium):
- If the attack hits, you may add 1 additional AP to the attack (once per All In).
- This additional AP does not further reduce your AC.
*Decisive, overwhelming strikes.*

#### Fractal Path 3
Lesser Fracture: All In (Heavy) — Cost: 250 Ascension
For every AP added to your attack through All In, you gain: +6 Damage; +2 Critical Threshold; +2 Epic
Critical Threshold. *Raw impact at extreme personal risk.*

Death-bound Swing — Greater Fracture: All In (Heavy) — Cost: 500 Ascension
When using All In (Heavy):
- If the attack lands as a Critical or Epic Critical, you ignore all incoming stance damage until your
  next turn.
- If the attack misses, enemies gain Small Advantage on attacks against you until your next turn.
*True masochistic risk: absolute payoff or punishment.*

### 11. Controlled Violence
*Power is not always loud.*
Once Per Turn
Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee attack and do not use All In, you may reduce the
attack's damage by 2. If you do, the target suffers Small Disadvantage on its next attack roll made
before the end of its next turn on you.

#### Fractal Path 1
Suppression Discipline — Cost: 250 Ascension
When using Controlled Violence, the target also suffers −2 Pace(s) until the end of its next turn.

Calculated Suppression — Cost: 500 Ascension

### 12. Unyielding Frame
*You are not easily moved.*
1 RP | once per round
When you would be forcibly moved against your will, you may spend 1RP
- Reduce the forced movement by 1 Pace.
- If you are wielding a shield, reduce the forced movement by 2 Paces instead.
- If you are wielding a Heavy shield, reduce the forced movement by 4 Paces instead.

### 13. Blood Rhythm
*Pain sharpens your tempo.*
If you gain Pressure while at or below half HP, you gain 1 additional Pressure.
When your Pressure reaches 3, on your next successful melee hit you can call forth your Blood Rhythm, and
cause your attack to deal +5 true Damage, and all Pressure is lost.

### 14. War Engine
*Crowds feed the fire. Their corpses raise your mountain for you are the king of it.*
At the start of your turn, if you are engaged with two or more creatures, on your last turn you gain 1AP.

### 15. Tandem With Death
*You can feel the hollowing drift of deaths rotten breath on your neck, but this only gives you more
resolve, as you steady yourself, and harden your spirit.*
At the start of your turn when you're at 25% of your maximum HP or less, you make a mighty display of
what it means to be a warrior. You gain a Temporary +1 AP and 5 Paces Of footspeed until then end of your
turn. In addition to this, you gain the ability to land Brutal Epic Critical hits When you are in Tandem
with death.

**Brutal Epic Critical hits** — A Brutal Epic-Critical hit deals your base damage twice, and applies your
Critical, and Epic-Critical damage a single time.

### 16. Dominance Protocol
*When weakness appears, you claim it; for it's the only time you get to witness it.*
While engaged with a creature whose Stance is broken, you gain +1 to hit against that creature.

#### Fractal Path 1

Collapse the Weak — Cost: 500 Ascension

### 17. Relentless Pursuit
*You do not let prey escape your sight, until you've sent them home to their god.*
When a creature within your Presence disengages from an ally (not you), you may spend 1RP
- Move up to 4 Pace toward that creature.
- This movement does not provoke disengage-based reactions from any creature.

### 18. Hardened Advance
*Killing is not an end. For your only true goal is motion on the field.*
When you reduce a creature to 0 HP during your turn, you may move 5 Pace immediately.
This movement does not consume your natural foot speed.

### 19. War Discipline
*Control precedes destruction. For you can expend yourself to extinguish your foe's actions.*
Once per round
When you successfully land an Epic-Crit on a creature with a melee attack, you may spend: All your
remaining resources. The target cannot use Lair Actions until the start of its next turn.

### 20. Commanding Presence
*Your presence bends the battlefield, your might is still noticeable that your allies can physically
feel it.*
Allies within 20 Paces of you gain:
- +2 to all resistances
- Small Advantage on the first reaction roll they make each combat.
Additionally, once per Stabilization Rest, when you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you regain:
1AP | 1BP | 1RP : You cannot exceed your maximum in any resource through this effect.
