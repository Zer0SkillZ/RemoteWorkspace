# Incoming notes, part 9: Templar Augmentation — Grafted by Nox
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept.)

## NOX
God of Redemption, Judgment, and Execution

### 2. Fracturing of the Lock 10 percent
When you Open your Lock, faint fractures of golden light spread beneath your skin. A thin, cracked halo
flickers above your head, unstable and incomplete. Your eyes glow softly gold. Your voice grows colder,
more measured.

**Ability: Judgment Brand**
2 Chakra | Additional 2 damage per 2 Chakra
- When you make a weapon attack, you may choose to empower it with Judgment.
- That attack deals plus 2 Divine Damage in addition to its normal damage.
- You may declare this per attack.

### 4. Fracturing of the Lock 20 percent
The halo stabilizes, though it remains visibly cracked. Small golden protrusions press at your temples,
the earliest hint of horns. Your posture shifts slightly forward, as if weighed by unseen authority.

**Ability: Light of Discernment**
2 Chakra | Once Per encounter
- You gain advantage on checks to detect deception, corruption, or concealed hostile intent.
- Your Divine Damage ignores RES.

### 6. Fracturing of the Lock 30 percent
Your pupils narrow into burning slits of judgment. The golden fractures deepen across your skin,
spreading like divine fault lines. Your armor begins to fuse subtly against your flesh.

**Ability: Executioner Oath**
Passive
- When you hit a creature that is below half Health, deal plus 2 Divine Damage.
- If the creature is Evil, this bonus becomes plus 4 Divine Damage.

### 8. Fracturing of the Lock 40 percent
The horns break fully from your temples, curved and metallic gold. The halo crackles constantly,
shedding faint motes of radiant dust. Your hands elongate slightly, fingers sharpening at the tips.

**Ability: Steed of Revelation**
1AP | 6 Chakra
- You may summon a Divine Steed.
- The steed has double your current movement.
- Mounting or dismounting costs 1 BP.
- The steed vanishes when your Lock closes.

### 10. Fracturing of the Lock 50 percent
Half your being now belongs to judgment. Black and gold shards begin to grow across your shoulders and
spine. Your armor is partially absorbed, layered into your body like divine plating.

**Ability: Radiant Sentence**
1AP | 6 Chakra
- You can mark a creature with the Mark of Impending Sentencing.
- Your Grafted Mark triggers, when that creature takes Divine damage
- The mark inflicts 2 Divine Damage per instance of damage when triggered.

### 12. Fracturing of the Lock 60 percent
Your voice echoes faintly as if spoken by more than one presence. Your feet distort, claws forming from
radiant bone. Golden veins pulse beneath darkened skin.

**Ability: Beacon of Redemption**
Passive
- Allies within 5 Paces gain plus 1 AC against Evil creatures.
- When you heal one ally within 30 paces heals for Half the of what you heal.

### 14. Fracturing of the Lock 70 percent
Your spine arches into a permanent forward hunch, imposing and predatory. Shards of black and gold
crystal erupt along your forearms. Your shadow fractures behind you like stained glass.

**Ability: Final Measure**
- Your Divine Damage ignores Immunity to Divine Damage when attacking creatures that are not Ascended /
  Legendary creatures.

### 16. Fracturing of the Lock 80 percent
Emotion drains from your expression entirely. The halo burns bright and violently fractured, lightning
arcing across its broken circumference. Your claws lengthen and your skin hardens into divine chitin.

**Ability: Divine Retribution**
Passive
- Each time you spend 1 AP, a bolt of Divine energy strikes the closest hostile creature within 8 Pace
  radius.
- It deals 4 Divine Damage.
- Against Evil creatures, this damage is doubled.
- This may trigger once per AP used.

### 18. Fracturing of the Lock 90 percent
You are barely recognizable. Your armor is fully integrated into your form, fused into jagged divine
plating. Your horns extend long and regal. Golden light leaks constantly from the fractures in your body.

**Ability: Voice of the Tribunal**
Passive
- When you reduce a creature to 0 Health, regain 3 Chakra.
- Allies within 4 Paces gain plus 2 EC until the start of your next turn.

### 20. Fracturing of the Lock 99 percent
The Lock shatters completely.
You are no longer yourself.
A fully fractured, blazing golden halo spins above your head, crackling with divine energy. Massive curved
golden horns crown your skull. Your body is encased in interlocking shards of black and radiant gold
crystal, your former armor entirely consumed and reforged into living divine plating. Your posture is
permanently hunched, predatory and inevitable. Your hands are fully clawed, elongated into instruments of
execution. Your feet end in taloned digits that scar the ground with radiant fractures wherever you stand.
Your eyes burn like twin suns without warmth.
You are the Avatar of Judgment.

**Ability: Avatar of Judgment**
Passive
While your Lock is Open:
- Your form fully transforms into the Avatar of Nox temporarily.
- Your weapon attacks deal plus 10 Divine Damage.
- Your Divine Damage ignores RES and IMM.

**Judgment Absolute**
- Once per turn, when you deal Divine Damage to a creature, you may declare it Condemned.
- A Condemned creature become easier to hit reducing their effective AC by 2.
- If the creature is Evil, it instead takes double that amount.

**Execution Decree**
- If a creature is reduced below 25 percent Health by your Divine Damage, you may immediately make one
  additional weapon attack against it without spending AP.
- This may occur once per turn.

While Avatar of Judgment is active:
- You gain plus 2 AC and plus 4 EC.
- You are immune to Fear.
- Your presence counts as both Divine and Overwhelming for all effects.

When your Lock closes:
- You suffer 1 level of fatigue.
