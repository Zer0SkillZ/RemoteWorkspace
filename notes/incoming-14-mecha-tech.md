## Class Identity
The Mecha-Tech is a mechanical mastermind—part tactician, part engineer, part living interface. They do
not rely on raw physical prowess or traditional spellcasting, but instead on intelligence-driven force,
leveraging a bonded mechanical construct (The Mech) as both weapon and armor.
No two MechaTechs are identical. Some favor brute mechanical output, others precision tools,
arcane-infused systems, or experimental energy cores. What unites them is obsession: perfect ratios,
modular design, and relentless iteration.
The MechaTech is two entities acting as one—operator and machine—customized through nodes, fracture paths,
and augmentations.

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Standard class progression.
- Reaction Points (RP): Used primarily for Mech-based reactions.
- Overdrive (Class Resource): Generated through combat activity and spent to power Mech systems.
- Integrity: Represents Mech structural health; distinct from HP, when gaining health from this class, its
  granted instead of health.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on first class)
- Armor: Medium, Heavy, Super-Heavy
- Weapons: Integrated weaponry
- Tool mastery: Craftsmen-Tools, Engineering kits
- Stat Focus: Intelligence, Strength

## Level Progression Table
(¤ marks appear before levels 4 and 6 in the source — possibly Augmentation nodes.)

### Level 1 – Mech Synchronization
You construct and bond with a Personal Mech Frame.
- Your Mech is in a fully functioning state and your now able to use it in the field
  - Your base movement, Integrity, Overdrive added to sheet
- You and the Mech share positioning and initiative.
- If your Mech is disabled, you can't use any of its online features, and it can only move 5 ft per round.
  - Your maximum Overdrive per round is now set to 1
  - Your maximum modular slots is 1
  - Your maximum Cores slots is 1 with a maximum of 1
  - Your maximum complexity is 2
you can change out one module for stabilization rest
  - You can fully reorientate your mech during a light or heavy rest
See MechaTech Modular Page for parts

### Level 2 – Modular Architecture
Your Mech is designed for change.
- Unlock more Module Slots (max set to 2).
- You may swap modules during Heavy-rests.

### Level 3 – Surge Engine
You have learned to push beyond safe operating limits.
- You gain the ability to overclock your power source once per encounter gaining back all lost, or used,
  Action points.
  - After using surge, you gain the flat-footed effect
    - AC modifier halved, movement halved until your next turn.

### ¤ Level 4 – System Calibration
You refine how machine and mind communicate.
You can now leave and enter your mech for 1 BP instead of 1 AP as your mech is designed to instantly
recognize you biosignature from a distance.

### Level 5 – Integrated Weapon Platform
Your Mech becomes a true combat extension.
- You have gained an advanced Modular Slot on your mech. (1)
- Your Maximum complexity is increased to 5

### ¤ Level 6 – Emergency Protocols
You install autonomous safety routines.
- When your mech takes critical damage, you can use 1 Overdrive point, and 1 BP to eject yourself from
  your mech, making yourself immune to non-ranged attacks until your next turn as you're launched into a
  safe air space with a parashoot within 45ft

### Level 8 – Field Reconfiguration
You can adapt mid-combat.
- Once per encounter you can hot-swap a modal with a 25% chance to fail, with hot swop lasts for 2 rounds
  before it reverts.
- You can hot swap any module piece with its corresponding module slot type.

### Level 9 – Reinforced Chassis
Your Mech becomes harder to break as you have specialized its armoring from experience
You gain 5 points of resistance to spread amongst your resistances.
You gain 1 point to add to your immunities from one of the following
- Heat resistance (Immune to Fire damage)
- Cold Resistance (Immune to Cold damage)
- Perfect sealing (Immune to Poisoned effect)
- Perfected Hardening (Immune to Bludgeoning damage)
- Perfected armor angling (Immune to Piercing damage)
- Hard Points Perfected (Immune to Bleed damage)
These Resistances/Immunities only apply when in your mech.

### Level 10 – Dual-Thread Control
You have been able to modify your mech to fit two cores allowing for the superior configuration.
- This can support a Dule-Core, or even two single cores

### Level 11 – Autonomous Subroutines
Your Mech gains limited independence.
- Your mech can now give live feedback to you when your within 1mile through AI camera or senser
  interfaces, Identifying this such as.
  - Movement withing area
  - Specific creatures, or objects moving within focus area
  - Sounds (Including transmitting sound to you telepathic or technological)
In addition, when your mech is online, you can call it to you, allowing it to move up to 25ft to your
location, or follow you.
