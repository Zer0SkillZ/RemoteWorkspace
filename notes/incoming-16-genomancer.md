# Incoming notes, part 16: Genomancer
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept.
No identity / resources / proficiencies block was given yet.)

### 1. Genetic Initiation
You begin rewriting your own biology, imperfect but functional.
- You gain access to Simple Splice
- You may maintain 1 Active Simple Splice
- You may store 2 Simple Splices

Genetic Complexity Interaction:
- Simple Splice uses 0.5× GC

Favored Typing:
- Choose 2 Creature Typings
- When interacting with Favored Typings:
  - Effective GC is reduced by Node Level

Maximum Geno-Mutations:
- 3 mutations per day, recovering 1 per stabilization rest.

### 2. Expanded Genome
Your body stabilizes additional foreign structures.
- Increase stored Simple Splices by +1
- You may maintain +1 Active Simple Splice

### 3. Functional Extraction
You begin extracting usable biological actions.
- You gain access to Advanced Splice
- You may maintain 1 Active Advanced Splice
- You may store 1 Advanced Splice
Genetic Complexity Interaction:
- Advanced Splice uses 1.0× GC

### 4. Genome Expansion
Your internal biological framework grows in capacity.
- Increase stored Simple Splices by +1
- Increase stored Advanced Splices by +1

### 5. Partial Assimilation
You begin meaningfully adopting foreign biological performance.
- When using Simple or Advanced Splice:
  - You gain 1/2 of the difference between your stat and the creature's stat (rounded down) to the max of
    the creatures.

### 6. Dual Function Integration
You can sustain multiple functional biological systems.
- You may maintain +1 Active Advanced Splice

### 7. Deep Storage
Your genome can retain more complex structures.
- Increase storage of all Splice types by +1

### 8. Co-Efficiency
Your systems refine without expanding.
Your maximum Geno-Mutations per day is increased by +2

### 9. Advanced Assimilation
You begin approaching full biological integration.
- When using Simple or Advanced Splice:
  - You gain 2/3 of the difference between your stat and the creature's stat (rounded down) to the max of
    the creatures

### 10. Full Genetic Rewrite
You no longer imitate. You become.
- You gain access to Full Mutation

**Full Mutation:**
- Uses 2.0× GC
- You fully replace: Stats; Actions; Traits (IMM / RES / WK); Movement; Senses
Restrictions:
- You may not maintain Simple or Advanced Splices during Full Mutation
HP Rule:
- Your HP = 10 × Genomancer Nodes ~ Default health pool
- HP cannot exceed the creature's maximum HP

**Reinforced Form:**
- Choose 1 Full Mutation Form
- That form gains: +10 HP per 2500 Ascension Score
- Cannot exceed creature HP
- May be reassigned during Stabilization Rest

**Amalgam Splicing:**
- You may combine 2 creatures into a single form
Rules:
- GC = (Average of both creatures) × 2
- Treated as a single valid splice source
  - Stats, actions, and economy is decided by the game master.

**Natural Regeneration**
- At the start of your turn: Heal 2 HP
- If at 0 HP or below: Heal 5 HP instead
Restriction:
- Bloodied healing does not occur if you took damage while at 0 HP before your turn in a round.

### 11. Expanded Integration
You sustain broader biological complexity.
- Increase all storage by +1
- Increase active splice limits by +1

### 12. Mutation Endurance
You can maintain full transformations more frequently.
- Increase Full Mutation usage capacity by +1

### 13. Internal Optimization
Your body reduces inefficiencies.
Your maximum Geno-Mutations per day is increased by +2

### 14. Multi-System Operation
You can sustain additional active biological functions.
- Increase Active Advanced Splices by +1

### 15. Genetic Specialization
Your focus deepens.
- Gain +1 Favored Typing

### 16. Maximum Capacity Expansion
Your genome reaches extreme storage density.
- Increase storage and active limits by +1

### 17. Complete Assimilation
There is no difference between you and the source.
- Simple and Advanced Splice now use Full Stat Replacement (No longer use partial inheritance rules)

### 18. Sustained Mutation
You maintain transformation with greater consistency.
- Increase Full Mutation usage capacity +1

### 19. Reinforcement Scaling
Your perfected form continues to evolve.
- Reinforced Form continues scaling with Ascension Score

### 20. Apex Genome
You are no longer bound by natural biology.
- Gain +1 Favored Typing (Total: 4)
- Favored Typing Reduction: −Level (−20 at Level 20)
- Full Mutation retains all benefits with no additional restrictions
