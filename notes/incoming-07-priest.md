## Class Identity
Priests are divine martyrs who willingly carry the suffering of others. They intercept wounds, absorb
curses, and transform affliction into divine strength. Their miracles grow stronger the more burdens they
bear for their allies.

## Base Resources
- Action Points (AP): Extremely limited. Priests rely on deliberate casting and preparation rather than
  frequent actions. Most of their influence occurs through reactions.
- Reaction Points (RP): Primary resource for the class. Used to intercept damage, absorb burdens, shield
  allies, and trigger defensive miracles.
- Bonus Points (BP): Used sparingly for intense acts of martyrdom, sudden divine intervention, or
  amplifying protective miracles under extreme pressure.

## Recommended Proficiencies (Granted on First Class)
- Armor: Light Armor, Medium Armor
- Weapons: Simple Weapons, Ritual Weapons (such as staffs, maces, and ceremonial blades, Spell books)
- Tool Mastery: Scripture Kit, Ritual Components, Religious Implements
- Stat Focus: Charisma

## Core Class Nodes

### 1. Consume Burden
You learn the sacred act of suffering in place of another.
You gain the spell:

**Consume Burden**
Reaction | Sight | Verbal
Cost: 1 RP | PP varies
Range: Presence
When an allied creature within Presence gains a burden (debuff, curse, condition, or harmful effect), you
may transfer that burden to yourself. The burden functions exactly as written when applied to you.

Burden Restriction
- Consume Burden may only transfer Mundane burdens.
- You cannot transfer: Advanced burdens; Legendary burdens; Ascended burdens

Scripture Learning
If you consume a burden you have never carried before:
- You may only consume that specific burden once per day until it is learned.
During any rest (SR, Light, or Heavy), you may record that burden into your Scripture.
Once recorded:
- That burden is permanently learned and scribed.
- It may be consumed freely in the future also long as you have your scribes.
If a burden has multiple stages, each stage must be learned individually.

In addition, gain access to:
- Feeble Spells
- Level 1 Spells

### 2. Martyr's Reflex
Your instincts compel you to suffer in place of others.
Reaction
Cost: 1 RP | Sight | Vocal
When an ally within Presence takes HP damage, you may reduce the damage they take by up to 6, per 20
damage they taken and take the prevented damage yourself.

### 3. Divine Spellcasting II
You may now cast Level 2 Spells.

### 4. Shared Suffering
Your divine tolerance weakens burdens you carry.
Reaction
Cost: 1 BP
When you transfer a burden using Consume Burden, reduce its initial duration or intensity by 1 turn
before it applies to you to a minimum of 1 turn.

### 5. Divine Spellcasting III
You may now cast Level 3 Spells.

### 6. Sanctuary Reflex
Your faith creates momentary protection.
Reaction
Cost: 1 RP
When an ally within 2 paces | one Stride becomes the target of an attack, you may grant them a temporary
Divine shield equal to half your Divine Value they gain this Divine defensive until that attack resolves.

### 7. Divine Spellcasting IV

### 8. Burden Channeling

### 20. Living Martyr
You become a vessel of divine sacrifice.
While you carry burdens:
- Your healing spells are significantly empowered gaining a medium advantage on casting them; and true
  advantage on all rolled healing.
- Allies within Presence gain resistance to harmful effects.
Additionally: Once per encounter, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you may remain standing briefly and
immediately cast a spell without spending AP.
You may now cast Level 15 Spells.
