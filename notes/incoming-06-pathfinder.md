# Incoming notes, part 6: Pathfinder
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept. No identity /
resources / proficiencies block was given for this class yet.)

### 1. Favored Creatures
Choose one Favored Creature type.
When attacking a Favored Creature:
- +1 flat damage
You may identify a creature once per encounter for 1 BP
Favored Creatures unlock Predator Overrides beginning at Node 3.
In addition: Your maximum BP is increased to 2

### 2. Weapon Platform
Select one favored weapon format.
You have made a connection and found your favored format of weapon. Gain +1 Crit and +1 Epic-Crit when
attacking with that weapon format.
- Side Arm (Pistols)
- Short Assault (Semi SMG)
- Rapid Short Assault (Burst SMG)
- Infantry Assault (Semi Rifles)
- Rapid Assault (Burst Rifles)
- Marksman (Long-Ranged Precision Rifles)
- Thrown Weapons (Throwing Knives, Hatchets, Light Spears)
- Light Bows (Shortbows, Hunting Bows, Compact Recurves)
- Heavy Compound Bows (War-bows, Siege Bows, Mechanically Drawn Bows)
- Crossbows (Standard, Repeating, or Heavy Crossbows)

### 3. Predator Override I
1BP
When attacking a Favored Creature, you may activate one Override option per attack.
Override Options:
- 1 BP - Ignore cover.
- 2 BP - Treat Long Range as in range.
- 2 BP - Ignore positioning-based Disadvantage.
- 3 BP - Target is treated as surprise attack for this attack.
Only one Override may be applied per attack at this level.

### 4. Prey Vectoring
1BP | Once per round
When a Favored Creature moves:
- 1 BP Reposition up to 1 Stride without provoking reactions.
In addition: Your maximum BP is increased to 3

### 5. Anatomical Mapping
When you Epic-Crit a Favored Creature, choose one:
- Suppress its Reaction until the end of its next turn.
- Reduce its movement by half during its next turn.
- Disable one non-legendary passive ability until the end of its next turn.

### 6. Kill Corridor
3 BP
Create a Kill Corridor between you and one Favored Creature for 1 round. While active:
- The target cannot benefit from cover against you Other than Full cover.
- You can Ignore Environmental penalties between you and the target.
- If the target moves Away, it provokes one ranged attack from you (once).

### 7. Specialization
Variable
Choose one:
- **Executioner** (1BP | Once Per round): You deal an additional 5 damage to creatures below 50% hp.
- **Stalker** (Once Per encounter): Your first attack for the encounter is always a crit with a 50/50 to
  epic crit.
- **Suppressor** (1RP | Once Per Round): Once Per round You can knock a non-magical projectile off course,
  succeeding if your hit value is equal to or higher than the attackers.
- **Harrier** (Passive): when you are Higher in elevation then a creature you gain a small advantage on
  attack rolls.

### 8. Favored Doctrine II
Choose a second Favored Creature type.
Against all Favored Creatures:
- Flat damage bonus increases to +2.

### 9. Override Expansion
You gain access to the following additional Override options: (Node 3)
- 2 BP : Ignore 1 source of Resistance for this attack.
- 3 BP | Once per round : Force the target to reroll a successful save against one of your effects.

### 10. Relentless Angle
2 RP | Once per encounter
If a Favored Creature disengages or breaks line of sight: Reposition up to 4 Strides and make one ranged
attack.
In addition: Your maximum RP is increased to 2

### 11. Bonus Pressure
When a Favored Creature misses you:
- Gain 1 BP Once per round
When you reduce a Favored Creature to 0 HP:
- Gain 2 BP.

### 12. Predator Override II
You may now activate up to two Overrides on the same attack. BP costs are cumulative.

### 13. Structural Weakness
2 BP | Once Per Round
When attacking a Favored Creature once per round you can reduce the target's AC by -2 for that attack.

### 14. Favored Doctrine III
Choose a third Favored Creature type.
Against all Favored Creatures:
- Flat damage bonus increases to +3.
- +1 Crit.

### 15. Pathfinder's Exposure
Before making an attack against a Favored Creature, you may declare Pathfinder's Exposure.
Effects if your attack hits Pathfinder's Exposure is applied.

**Pathfinder's Exposure**
- Targets stance is considered Broken until their turn.
- Actions with bonus points attached to them cost one less BP (minimum 1).
- +1 Crit.
In addition: Your maximum BP is increased to 4

### 16. Battlefield Rewrite
4 BP | Once per encounter
For 1 round against Favored Creatures:
- Treat Long Range as In range.
- Ignore all Resistance.
- Ignore positioning penalties.
- The target cannot gain Advantage against you.
- You are treated as above Higher in elevation when making attacks.

### 17. Exploit Instinct
When a Favored Creature becomes:
- **Vulnerable** (Set easily prone to illness or effects; All resist saves are made with a small
  disadvantage)
- **Staggered** (Stumbled or struggling to stand straight due to impact halved movement; impact and
  bludgeoning damage is doubled)
- **Prone** (Laying down or flat on their back; all melee attacks gain a medium advantage all ranged
  attack gain a small disadvantage)
- **Restrained** (incapable of moving arms and or legs; All attacks gain a medium advantage)
- **Dazed** (concussed and are starstruck do that hit to the head or bright lights; melee attacks are
  made at a small disadvantage, ranged attacks are made with a medium disadvantage)
- **Flat-Footed** (stumbling and unable to maintain stable footing; movement costs are doubled)
- **Inebriated** (drunk or high something of the like; all rolls are made with a small disadvantage)
Your next attack against that creature:
- +2 Crit +2 Epic-Crit
- On hit, refund 1 AP.

### 18. Override Mastery
All Override costs are reduced by 1 BP (minimum 1).
In addition, you gain 2 more overrides:
- 1BP - Pinning Shot : On a successful hit the creature Losses its movement until your next round.
- 1BP - Dazing Shot : On a successful hit the creature is set dazed as your shot hits it.

### 19. Absolute Lock
4 BP | Once per day
Lock one Favored Creature for 1 round. While Locked:
- It cannot benefit from cover against you.
- It cannot negate your applied Disadvantages.
- It cannot ignore your effects through Immunity unless Legendary.
- You gain 1 small advantage on them for each additional attack you make on them.

### 20. Capstone — Apex Predator: Total Engagement
Once per day, for 1 round:
- All creatures are treated as Favored.
- All Override options cost 1 BP.
- One Override is free per attack
- Your maximum AP Temporally increases by 1.
- Once during this round, you may: Convert a Hit to Critical; Or convert a Critical to Epic-Critical
