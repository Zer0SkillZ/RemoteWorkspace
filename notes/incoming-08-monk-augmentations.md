## Node 1. The Philosophy of the Great Eldor Spirit walker (AUG): per-tradition variants

### Wilhelm's Transcendent Palm
- You have gained the ability to gain **Momentous combo** at a combo maximum of 3
  - You gain your momentum by successfully landing different hit.
  - Your combo represents the ppmaximum about of combat buffs you can have on you during combat.
  - You lose one Momentous combo point, losing your oldest buff first, when going 2 or more rounds without
    landing an attack.
  - Once you add an attack into your combo it can't be removed by choice.
  - All combo expires at the end of an encounter
- **Wilhelms Combo Striking technics**
  - Simple Elbow Strike | 1Ap | 2 Bludgeoning damage |
    Simple Elbow Srike adds a Flat +1 damage to all of your unarmed attack damage when it's in your combo
  - Simple Strike | 1Ap | ½ strength to a maximum of 5 Bludgeoning damage |
    Simple Strike adds a +1 to all your unarmed attack when it's in your combo
  - Simple Kick | 1Ap | 2 Bludgeoning damage +1 per time used in a row to a maximum of 5 Bludgeoning damage |
    Simple Kick adds a +1 temporary Momentous combo Cap to a max of +1

### Sun Tzu's Endless Staff
- You have gained the ability to gain **Tempo combo** at a combo maximum of 3
  - You gain your momentum by successfully landing different hit, or by dodging an attack.
  - Your combo is used as an additional resource.
  - You lose –1 EC and –1 AC per 2 Tempo combo you actively have.
  - You gain +1 Flat damage per active Tempo combo you have, damage bonus is applied before expending your
    combo.
  - You lose only lose Tempo combo If you go 2 or more rounds without landing an attack.
    - When you lose Tempo combo in this way, you lose all Tempo combo.
  - All combo expires at the end of an encounter.
- **Sun Tzu's Combo Technics**
  - Sun Tzu's Tower Strike | 1 Combo | Flat +1 damage |
    When in combat you can Climb your weapon and gain that weapons Hight as if it were your own.
  - *Dawn-Breaker* (sub-heading in the notes)
    - Falling Strike | 1 Combo | Flat +1 damage to a maximum of +8 |
      When in combat fall to the ground adding your fall distance to your attack at +1 damage per foot
      Follen. Follen distance is calculated as half total Hight from head to ground.
    - Star Stepping | 3 Combo + 3 AP | 6 Bludgeoning damage +1 flat per pace traveled from target to target |
      Dexterity attack, gaining an additional small advantage per target attacked (1 | 2 | 3) small
      advantages.
      - When in combat with at least two enemies or more with less then 4 paces separating them, you can
        use your star stepping technic.
      - As you launch yourself into the air, you kick off each enemies head or face, bouncing off up to
        three targets using your staff as a pivot point; dealing 6 Bludgeoning damage, and accumulating +1
        flat damage as you accumulate more distance traveled apply it to the oncoming damage.

### Zhuangzi's Butterfly Chains (Psi power = ½ Psi level)
- You have gained the ability to gain **Psionic combo** at a combo maximum of 3
  - your psionic connection strengthens by successfully landing different hits and dodging hits. Hits
    grant 1, critical hits grant 2, and evading a hit grants 3 Psionic combo
  - Your Psionic combo is your gateway into psionic powers. Your flow creates a like with your mind opening
    your metaphysical gate way and connection to the dream and spirit Planes.
  - You lose one Psionic combo point, when you take elemental damage.
  - All combo expires at the end of an encounter
- **Zhuangzi's Combo Technics**
  - Unstable Shadow Strike | 2 Combo + Attack Action | no inherent damage |
    - You metaphysically reach into the spirit realm and open up a gate way, as you do a Shadow Double of
      yourself walks through the unseen gateway, genetically identical to yourself, but with half a soul and
      fluctuating in and out of existence.
    - As it steps out, it lounges at a target with you, allowing you to roll twice and take the better of
      the two rolls.
    - both of you use the same attack
    - The Unstable shadow clone can only deal a maximum of 10 damage.
    - The clone dissipates back to the Spirit realm as soon as the strike resolves.
  - Broken Soul's Mockery | 1 Combo 1 AP | 3 Psi damage | once per round |
    - You connect to spirit realm and call forth a broken soul, a being with nothing but anger and vitriol
      left in its soul. As you call it forth it mocks you target internally in their own voice in any
      language.
      - The target must have an intelligence of at least 0 or higher, and be capable of speaking and
        understanding a language.
      - Targeted creature must pass a DC 2 + your Psi power (Psi) or take 3 damage and suffer a small
        disadvantage on their next attack roll.
      - If the target critically fails they take 6 Psi damage, and suffer a medium disadvantage on their next
        attack.

### Aurelius' Unbroken Mountain
- You have gained the ability to gain **Explosive combo** at a combo maximum of 3
  - You sacrifice 2 of your AP for 2 RP
  - You gain your combo by being hit, evading hits, or through abilities.
  - Hits against you grant 1 combo per criticality, evading a hit grants 2 combo, or by using abilities
    like Feather in the Wind.
  - Your combo is used as an additional resource.
  - You gain an additional +2 temp hp per 3 max combo
  - You lose only lose Explosive combo If you go 2 or more rounds without being attacked.
    - When you lose Explosive combo in this way, you lose all Explosive combo.
  - All combo expires at the end of an encounter.
- **Aurelius's Combo Technics**
  - Pulsing Mountain Strike | 2 Combo + 1 AP | 15 Force damage |
    You lay your Weight into a heroic strike; relinquishing yourself from the punishment of defense, and
    striking down your foe with a singular powerful palm strike to their heart.
  - Feather in the Wind | 1 RP | NA |
    Your spirit lifts from your body and comforts and emboldens an ally for 2 rounds
    - Whilst your spirit is enshrouding an ally, they are protected by your temporary heath
    - Any attacks on your spirit can gain you Explosive combo as if the attacks were on you.
  - Clearing in the Mist | 1RP | NA |
    You focus on an ally's strike and your body instantly memories the strike, creating a perfect guide for
    you to follow.
    - After using Clearing in the Mist you can use your ally's attack roll as if it were your own, as long
      as your striking the same creature.
