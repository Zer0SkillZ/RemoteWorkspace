# Incoming notes, part 4: Scoundrel
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept. No identity /
resources / proficiencies block was given for this class yet.)

### 1. Twin Cut Doctrine
The Scoundrel has mastered fighting with two light weapons as a single, fluid motion.
Passive
When wielding two light melee weapons, you may make one attack that strikes with both your primary and
off-hand weapons.
- The primary weapon deals full damage
- The off-hand weapon deals half damage
- Both hits are resolved as part of the same attack
Restrictions:
- You cannot spend BP to make off-hand attacks
- This node replaces the normal dual-wield BP rule for you
- You cannot gain additional off-hand attacks from any source
This attack follows all normal hit, critical, and stance interaction rules.

### 2. Measured Strike
You learn to read opponents mid-fight, adjusting force instead of overcommitting.
Passive
After you successfully resolve a hit against a creature, you may make an Insight Skill Check to analyze
their defenses.
- SC = ⌈½ the creature's Level to a minimum of 5⌉
- Add your Scoundrel Level as an additional modifier to this check
On a success:
- You learn whether removing your attack modifier from this attack would still result in a Stance Hit
- If it would, you may immediately remove your attack modifier
- The attack is resolved as a Stance Hit instead of its original result
Mastery:
- Once you succeed against a specific creature, you no longer need to make this check against that
  creature for the remainder of the encounter to use this passive.
This effect can only be attempted after a successful hit and does not apply to misses.

### 3. More than Blade
You are more than the blade, and arrow, you have found an atonement to the Aether, and have found your
arcane reservoir.
Passive
You gain 2+ your intelligence modifier as maximum mana.
- Gain 2 feeble Scoundrel spells.
- Gain 1 Level one Scoundrel Spell

### 4. Tactical Redirection
Dismantling is more important to you than damaging.
Passive
When you successfully land a Hit, you may choose to reduce the Health damage dealt by half.
If you do, you can apply that damage as stance damage.
This decision is made after the attack roll resolves.

### 5. Calculated Exploitation
You learn how to turn imperfect strikes into decisive pressure.
1 CaP
When you land a Glancing Blow / Stance Hit, or intentionally redirect a hit into one, you may spend 1
Critical Action to make an additional attack roll against the same creature.
- On a Critical Hit, apply your Critical damage bonus as flat damage to the creature's Stance
- This roll does not replace the original hit
Additional Effect:
- You gain 2 Critical Actions per day
If an Epic Critical is rolled but you do not have an ability that allows Epic conversion, only the
Critical bonus is applied.

### 6. Structural Study
Once you choose your target you tend to persist upon it.
Passive
When you deal Stance damage to a creature:
- Mark them until the end of your next turn.
- Your attacks against a Marked creature deals +2 Stance damage.
- A creature may only be Marked by you once at a time.

### 7. Breakpoint Awareness
You have mastered the breaking point and you dwell in it
1 CaP
When a creature you have damaged falls below half of its maximum Stance:
- Your next attack against that creature gains Small Advantage.
- This effect may trigger once per creature per encounter.
In addition:
- You gain 2+ your intelligence modifier as maximum mana.
- Gain 2 Feeble Scoundrel spells.
- Gain 1 Level One Scoundrel Spell
- Gain 1 Level Two Scoundrel Spell

### 8. Suppressive Rhythm
Making a delicate melee attack, as you target the weakest points of your target.
1AP
If this attack deals Stance damage: The target suffers –2 Pace(s) of FS until the start of your next turn.
If this attack results in a Stance Hit, increase the reduction to –4 Pace(s) of.

### 9. Compounded Pressure
Each time you deal Stance damage to the same creature on consecutive turns:
Increase that Stance damage by +1, stacking up to a maximum equal to your Half Scoundrel Level.
Stacks reset if:
- You fail to deal Stance damage on your turn
- You attack a different creature

### 10. Calculated Mercy
Your understanding of timing and anatomy allows you to gamble for devastating breaks.
2 CaP
When using Pressure Point Exploitation, you may spend 2 Critical Actions instead of 1 to attempt an Epic
Critical Stance conversion.   ← "Pressure Point Exploitation" may be an old name for node 5
- On a Critical Hit: apply the Critical bonus to Stance
- On an Epic Critical Hit: apply the Epic Critical bonus to Stance
If the Epic threshold is not met, resolve normally based on the roll.
Additional Effect:
- You gain 2 additional Critical Actions per day

### 11. Fractured Guard (Fracture Node)
Passive
When a creature has taken Stance damage from you this turn: They suffer –1 AC against your allies
attacks. This effect ends at the start of your next turn.

#### Fractal Path 1
(1) Lesser Fracture: Extended Exposure — Cost: 250 Ascension
When a creature is affected by Fractured Guard: Increase the AC penalty to –2 against your allies attacks.

(2) Greater Fracture: Lingering Fracture — Cost: 500 Ascension
When a creature is affected by Fractured Guard: The AC penalty lasts until the end of your next turn
instead of the start.

#### Fractal Path 2
(1) Lesser Fracture: Shared Collapse — Cost: 250 Ascension
When an ally hits a creature affected by Fractured Guard: That ally deals +1 Stance damage.

(2) Greater Fracture: Cascading Break — Cost: 500 Ascension
When an ally reduces a creature affected by Fractured Guard to 0 Stance: That creature suffers Small
Disadvantage on all attack rolls until its next turn.

#### Fractal Path 3
(1) Lesser Fracture: Focused Weak Point — Cost: 250 Ascension
Fractured Guard may now apply to only one creature per turn. If it applies to only one creature: Increase
the AC penalty to –3 against your allies attacks.

(2) Greater Fracture: Terminal Exposure — Cost: 500 Ascension
When a creature affected by Fractured Guard takes damage from an ally: They suffer –4 Pace(s) FS until the
start of your next turn.

### 20. Who decided That?
You have cheated long enough, you might as well blatantly cheat reality with uncanny efficiency.
Once Per Day
Once per day, you may invoke one of the following effects after results are revealed:

**Decided Miss** — When you are about to be hit by an attack, after damage is determined, you may declare
that the attack misses instead. The attack is treated as a miss for all purposes.

**Decided Hit** — When you miss with an attack, you may declare that the attack instead counts as an Epic
Critical Hit.
- Resolve the attack as a normal Epic Critical
- This may bypass all normal hit failure states

**Decided Disorientation** — When you land an Epic-Critical Hit, you may choose to convert it into a Full
Stance Hit, ignoring all stance damage reductions.
- The Epic Critical damage is applied entirely to Stance
- No halving or reduction applies

Each time you use any of the above effects, you gain 1 point of Overconfidence.

**Overconfidence** — Your certainty begins to betray you. For each point of Overconfidence you have:
- You suffer Small Disadvantage on (these disadvantages stack): All Attack Rolls; All Skill Checks
- You are also effected by Scoundrels Hubris

**Scoundrels Hubris** — When you successfully land a hit while suffering from Overconfidence: The total
disadvantage value applied to that attack is instead dealt as True damage to the target. This damage:
- Is applied after the hit resolves
- Ignores armor, resistances, and reductions
- Does not apply on a miss

**Reset Condition**
- All Overconfidence is removed at the end of a heavy Rest
- Overconfidence cannot be removed by other effects unless explicitly stated
