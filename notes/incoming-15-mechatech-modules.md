# Incoming notes, part 15: MechaTech Modules
(Saved while waiting for ☆Go. Not yet applied to the app. Original wording kept.
Fields per module: Complexity, Visual, Activation / Cost, Effect or Attack, Execution, Ascension Scaling.
Advanced weapon modules 3–10 have no Visual line in the source.)

## SUPPORT MODULES

### 1. Auto-Repair Nanoforge — Complexity (1)
- Visual: A cluster of articulated injector arms fold out from the Mech's torso, glowing with soft internal light as nanites circulate.
- Activation / Cost: 2 AP, 1 Overdrive
- Effect: Restore 6 Integrity to the Mech or remove 1 mechanical impairment.
- Execution: The Mech seals ruptures mid-combat as liquid metal stitches damage shut.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 1500 Ascension, the Integrity restored increases by +2.

### 2. Kinetic Dampening Field — Complexity (2)
- Visual: Circular emitters along the Mech's frame project faint, overlapping energy ripples.
- Activation / Cost: Reaction, 1 RP
- Effect: Reduce incoming physical damage by 4, or convert up to 6 damage into Stance loss instead of Integrity.
- Execution: The Mech absorbs the impact, dispersing force outward in a muted shockwave.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 2500 Ascension, the damage reduced or converted increases by +2.

### 3. Tactical Relay Node — Complexity (1)
- Visual: A rotating sensor crown deploys above the Mech's head, projecting faint targeting glyphs.
- Activation / Cost: 1 AP
- Effect: Allies within 30 ft gain small advantage (+1d4) on attacks and checks until the start of your next turn.
- Execution: The Mech feeds predictive data to allies, guiding their movements with precision.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 2000 Ascension, the range increases by +10 ft.

### 4. Emergency Extraction Thrusters — Complexity (2)
- Visual: Compact thruster vents ignite along the Mech's back and legs with controlled bursts.
- Activation / Cost: Reaction, 1 RP
- Effect: Instantly reposition up to 15 ft or negate 1 forced-movement effect.
- Execution: The Mech launches sideways in a burst of controlled propulsion.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 2500 Ascension, the reposition distance increases by +5 ft.

### 5. Power Redistribution Core — Complexity (3)
- Visual: Exposed conduits pulse rhythmically as energy reroutes through the Mech's internal lattice.
- Activation / Cost: 1 OP
- Effect: Convert 1 Overdrive into 1 AP, 1 RP, or gain +4 temporary Stance until the start of your next turn.
- Execution: Power surges through secondary systems as the Mech rebalances its output on the fly.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 5000 Ascension, temporary Stance gained increases by +2.

## ADVANCED SUPPORT MODULES

### A1. Adaptive Reconstitution Matrix — Complexity (3)
- Visual: A dense lattice of hexagonal plates shifts constantly beneath translucent armor panels, glowing faintly as data streams across them.
- Activation / Cost: Reaction, 1 RP, 1 Overdrive
- Effect: Restore 8 Integrity, or convert up to 8 incoming damage into Stance loss.
- Execution: The Mech reconfigures its structure mid-impact, redistributing stress before failure occurs.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 5000 Ascension, Integrity restored and conversion cap each increase by +2.

### A2. Predictive Combat Lattice — Complexity (4)
- Visual: A holographic combat grid projects around the Mech, lines and vectors updating in real time.
- Activation / Cost: 1 AP
- Effect: You gain small advantage (+1d4) on attacks and reactions until the start of your next turn.
- Execution: The Mech anticipates enemy motion before it happens, acting on probability instead of instinct.
- Ascension Scaling: Every 10,000 Ascension, the granted advantage increases by one step (+1d4 → +1d6 → +1d8).
